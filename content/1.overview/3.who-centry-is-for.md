# Who Centry Is For

Centry is an exclusive network designed for regulated institutions that prioritise security and compliance. It is not an open marketplace; membership is curated to maintain the integrity of the intelligence.

## Target Audience

### 1. Financial Institutions (Banks & Credit Unions)
- **Role**: Assessing credit risk, preventing account takeovers, complying with AML/KYC.
- **Value**: Early warning of threats affecting other banks; reduced operational losses.

### 2. Neo-Banks & Fintechs
- **Role**: Rapid customer acquisition, digital-first onboarding.
- **Value**: preventing synthetic identities and bonus abuse without adding friction to the user experience.

### 3. Lenders (BNPL, Personal Loans)
- **Role**: Instant credit decisions.
- **Value**: Improved "thin file" assessment; detection of loan stacking.

### 4. Telecommunications Providers
- **Role**: Preventing subscription fraud and device theft.
- **Value**: Linking digital identity risk to physical device/SIM risk.

### 5. Payment Processors & Gateways
- **Role**: Securing transaction flows.
- **Value**: Velocity checks across merchants; identifying stolen card testing.

## Membership Requirements

To maintain the quality of our network, all partners must meet strict criteria:

### Regulatory Status
Partners must be regulated entities or operate under the supervision of a regulator (e.g., verified fintechs). We do not accept anonymous or unregulated businesses.

### Data Security
Partners must demonstrate ISO 27001 compliance (or equivalent) and pass a security review.

### Operational Capability
Partners must have the technical resource to integrate our API securely and the operational team to act on intelligence (and respond to network inquiries).

### Reciprocity
Partners must be willing to contribute signals. **We do not support "read-only" access**. The strength of the network comes from mutual contribution.
