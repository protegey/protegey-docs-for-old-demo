# Explicit Non-Use Cases

To ensure legal compliance and system integrity, we are explicit about what Centry is **not** designed for. Attempting to use the platform for these purposes is a violation of the Partner Agreement.

## 1. Not a Background Check Service

Centry analyzes **risk signals**, not personal histories.
- **DO NOT** use Centry to screen potential employees.
- **DO NOT** use Centry for tenant screening.
- **DO NOT** use Centry to determine insurance eligibility.

These activities are governed by the FCRA (in the US) and similar laws globally, which require specific dispute resolution mechanisms that Centry's risk model does not support.

## 2. Not a Marketing Data Source

Centry data is strictly for **fraud prevention and security**.
- **DO NOT** use Centry signals to target marketing campaigns.
- **DO NOT** use Centry to build customer profiles for advertising.
- **DO NOT** use Centry to "enrich" lead data for sales teams.

## 3. Not a Sole Determinant for Adverse Action

While Centry provides a powerful risk score, it should be one factor in a broader decisioning matrix.
- **DO NOT** automatically deny service based solely on a Centry score without a human review or multi-factor policy (unless the score indicates a confirmed blocklist match).
- **DO NOT** use Centry as the exclusive reason for closing an existing account without internal investigation.

## 4. Not for Personal surveillance

- **DO NOT** use the system to look up individuals for personal reasons.
- **DO NOT** query the system for celebrities, public figures, or acquaintances unless they are engaging in a legitimate transaction with your institution. All queries are audit-logged and monitored.

## 5. Not a "Black Box" Oracle

Partners are responsible for their own risk decisions. Centry provides intelligence, not indemnification. Partners must understand *why* a signal was flagged and ensure their use of that signal complies with their own internal policies and local laws.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="red"}
**Enforcement**: We employ active monitoring to detect misuse. Violation of these non-use cases will result in immediate suspension of connectivity and potential legal action.
::
