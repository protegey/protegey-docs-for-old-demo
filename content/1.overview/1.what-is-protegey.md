# What is Protegey

Protegey is a **trust-based fraud intelligence network** designed for financial institutions, fintechs, telcos, and government agencies.

## The Problem

Traditional fraud detection systems operate in isolation. Each institution maintains its own fraud database, missing critical signals from fraudsters who operate across multiple platforms.

## The Solution

Protegey enables **secure, privacy-preserving signal sharing** across trusted partners. When one partner detects suspicious activity, the network learns and protects all participants.

## Who It's For

- **Banks**: Detect account takeovers and fraudulent transactions
- **Fintechs**: Prevent identity fraud during onboarding
- **Telcos**: Identify SIM swap attacks and account abuse
- **Government**: Support AML/KYC compliance efforts

## What It Is NOT

- **Not a replacement** for existing fraud systems
- **Not a data aggregator** that owns your signals
- **Not a credit bureau** or identity verification service
- **Not a regulatory compliance solution** (it supports, not replaces)

## Core Principles

1. **Trust-based**: Partners are vetted and approved
2. **Privacy-first**: Data isolation and encryption by default
3. **Network effects**: Value increases with participation
4. **Regulatory aligned**: Designed to support AML/KYC requirements
