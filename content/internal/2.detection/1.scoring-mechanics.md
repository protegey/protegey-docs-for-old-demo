# Scoring Mechanics

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lock" color="red"}
**RESTRICTED**: Internal Protegey team only.
::

## Base Score Calculation

The risk score ($S$) is a normalized sigmoid function of the weighted signal sum ($W$).

$$
S = \frac{100}{1 + e^{-(W - T)}}
$$

Where:
- $W$ = Sum of (Signal Severity * Partner Trust * Time Decay)
- $T$ = Threshold constant (currently `5.0`)

## Time Decay

Signals decay exponentially over time to ensure old data doesn't permanently condemn an entity.

```python
decay = e ^ (-0.1 * days_since_event)
```

- **Half-Life**: ~7 days.
- **Max Age**: 90 days (effectively 0 weight).

## Tuning Policy

Changes to $T$ (Threshold) or the Decay Constant require **CTO approval** and a full backtest against the `golden_dataset`.
