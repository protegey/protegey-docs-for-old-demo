# Environments

Centry provides two completely isolated environments to ensure safe testing and reliable production operations.

| Feature | Sandbox (`cny_test_`) | Production (`cny_live_`) |
|---------|-----------------------|--------------------------|
| **Endpoint** | `https://sandbox-api.centry.ai` | `https://api.centry.ai` |
| **Data** | Synthetic / Mock | Real Intelligence |
| **SLAs** | Best Effort | 99.99% Uptime |
| **Rate Limits** | Low (100/min) | High (Tier-based) |
| **Cost** | Free | Billable |

## Strict Isolation

**Data never crosses the boundary.**
- A signal submitted to Sandbox **will not** affect Production scores.
- A Production entity **cannot** be queried in Sandbox (it will return 404).

## Choosing the Right Environment

### Use Sandbox When:
- Developing your integration.
- Running automated CI/CD tests.
- Training new staff on the dashboard.
- Testing error handling (Sandbox supports "magic inputs" to trigger errors).

### Use Production When:
- Processing real customer applications.
- Submitting confirmed fraud signals.
- Conducting live transaction monitoring.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-flask-conical" color="purple"}
**Tip**: Always start development in Sandbox. We recommend keeping a "Stage" version of your app pointed at Sandbox even after you go live.
::
