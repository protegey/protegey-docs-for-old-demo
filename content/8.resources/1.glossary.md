# Glossary

Definitions of core terms used throughout the Protegey platform and documentation.

## Core Concepts

### Signal
A discrete indicator of risk submitted by a partner. Example: "User ID 123 committed payment fraud."

### Entity
The subject of a signal or query. Common entities include `User`, `Device`, `IP Address`, `Email`, `Phone Number`, and `Payment Instrument`.

### Risk Score
A normalized value from 0-100 indicating the probability that an entity is fraudulent. Higher is riskier.

### Trust Score (Partner Trust)
An internal metric representing the reliability of a partner. Partners with high accuracy and uptime have higher Trust Scores, giving their signals more weight in the network.

## Fraud Typologies

### Synthetic Identity
An identity assembled from real and fake information (e.g., real SSN + fake name) to create a "new" credit file.

### Account Takeover (ATO)
Unauthorized access to a legitimate user's account, often via credential stuffing or phishing.

### Bust-Out (First-Party Fraud)
A legitimate user who establishes credit with the specific intent of maxing out limits and defaulting.

### Velocity Abuse
High-frequency activity (signups, transactions) within a short time window, characteristic of bot attacks.

## System Terms

### Sandbox
An isolated environment for testing integration. Data in Sandbox is synthetic and does not affect Production.

### Idempotency
The property that an operation can be applied multiple times without changing the result beyond the initial application. (Note: Many Protegey write operations are *not* idempotent by default to track velocity).

### False Positive
A legitimate user/transaction incorrectly flagged as fraud. We strive to minimize this rate (<1%).

### False Negative
A fraudulent user/transaction incorrectly accepted as legitimate.
