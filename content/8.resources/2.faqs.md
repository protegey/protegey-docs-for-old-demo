# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### How is Protegey different from a credit bureau?
Credit bureaus track creditworthiness (ability to pay). Protegey tracks fraud risk (intent to defraud). We do not store credit histories, loan balances, or FICO scores.

### Can I try Protegey for free?
We offer a Sandbox trial for qualified applicants. Due to the sensitivity of the network, we do not offer open public trials.

## Data & Privacy

### Do you store my customer's PII?
We use a **hashing** and **tokenization** strategy. We typically ingest hashed identifiers (SHA-256). If you send raw PII, we hash it at the edge. We minimize strict PII storage to the absolute minimum required for matching.

### Can I delete my data?
Yes. You can request deletion of signals you submitted. However, for network integrity, some anonymized patterns may be retained for model training where allowed by law.

## Integration

### How long does integration take?
Most partners complete Sandbox integration in less than 3 days. Production go-live depends on your internal testing cycles.

### Do you have SDKs?
Yes, for major languages (Node, Python, Java, Go). See the Partner Portal for downloads.

### What happens if the API goes down?
Our SLA is 99.99%. However, your client should always implement a "fail-open" or "fail-closed" default strategy based on your risk appetite if the API is unreachable.
