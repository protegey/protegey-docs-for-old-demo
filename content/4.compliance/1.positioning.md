# Compliance & Regulations

Protegey helps you navigate the complex landscape of financial crime regulations while respecting user privacy.

## AML / KYC Positioning

Protegey is an **Intelligence Source**, not a decision of record for KYC (Know Your Customer) or AML (Anti-Money Laundering).

- **We Provide**: Signals, risk scores, and device intelligence.
- **You Decide**: Whether to onboard a user, flag a transaction, or file a SAR (Suspicious Activity Report).

We enhance your existing compliance stack but do not replace your core identity verification procedures (checking IDs, documents, etc.).

## Regulatory Alignment

### PSD2 / SCA (Strong Customer Authentication)
Protegey helps reduce friction for legitimate users by providing **Transaction Risk Analysis (TRA)** signals. High-confidence "low risk" scores can help you qualify for SCA exemptions.

### GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
We operate on the basis of **Legitimate Interest** (fraud prevention) for data processing.
- We support automated data deletion requests.
- We do not use data for marketing or ad targeting.

## Audit Trails

Every decision made by Protegey is immutable and auditable.
- **Decision Logs**: Full history of inputs, scores, and policy results.
- **Change Management**: Audit logs for all policy changes and configuration updates in the Partner Portal.
