# Compliance Alignment

Protegey is designed to support, not replace, the compliance obligations of regulated financial institutions. We help you navigate complex AML/KYC landscapes while reducing fraud risk.

## AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

Protegey acts as a critical **intelligence layer** in your AML stack.

### Transaction Monitoring
Protegey provides real-time risk scores that can be fed into your Transaction Monitoring System (TMS).
- **Benefit**: Reduce false positives by excluding low-risk entities validated by the network.
- **Benefit**: Catch "cleaning" phases where money moves through mule accounts before exiting the system.

### SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) Support
Our detailed reason codes and network correlation data provide the evidence needed to file high-quality, defensible SARs.

## KYC (Know Your Customer) & CIP

Protegey supplements your Customer Identification Program (CIP).

- **Identity Verification**: We do not replace document verification (e.g., scanning a passport). Instead, we provide **Identity Reputation**.
- **The Gap**: A passport can be genuine but stolen. Protegey tells you if that genuine passport has been used to open 50 accounts in the last hour.

## Regulatory Cooperation

We actively engage with regulators to advocate for collaborative fraud defense.

### Information Sharing (USA Patriot Act 314(b))
For US partners, Protegey facilitates information sharing under Section 314(b) safe harbors.
- We provide the secure channel and structured data formats necessary to comply with FinCEN requirements.

### PSD2 / SCA (Europe)
Protegey's Transaction Risk Analysis (TRA) capabilities help partners utilize exemptions to Strong Customer Authentication (SCA).
- **Low Risk Analysis**: Real-time scoring allows you to route low-risk transactions through frictionless flows, improving user experience while remaining compliant.

## Audit Readiness

In a regulated environment, "because the algorithm said so" is not an acceptable answer. Protegey ensures you are always audit-ready.

### Explainability
We do not use "black box" deep learning for regulatory decisioning. Every risk score is accompanied by:
- **Reason Codes**: Human-readable explanations (e.g., `VELOCITY_HIGH`, `PREVIOUS_FRAUD_HISTORY`).
- **Contributing Factors**: Breakdown of what drove the score.

### Immutability
Every decision is logged and immutable.
- **Decision Logs**: You can retrieve the exact state of the world (inputs, scores, logic) at the moment a decision was made, even years later.
- **Model Governance**: We maintain strict version control of all risk models. You will never be scored by an "unknown" model version.
