Protegey is designed to support, not replace, the compliance obligations of regulated financial institutions. We help you navigate complex AML/KYC landscapes while reducing fraud risk.

## Risk & Compliance as a Service (RCaaS) — Enterprise

For Enterprise partners, Protegey includes a fully integrated **AML compliance engine** gated by the `risk_and_compliance` feature flag. This is not advisory tooling — it runs automatically on every ingested signal.

**What runs on every signal:**

- **Sanctions screening** — entity screened against UN, EU, and AU sanctions lists (updated daily)
- **PEP detection** — fuzzy name matching (≥85% similarity) against a curated PEP designations database
- **Transaction monitoring rules** — 4 AML rules evaluated: structuring, velocity breach, round-number transactions, high-risk jurisdiction (FATF grey/black list)
- **Geographic risk scoring** — FATF classification applied per country code in the signal

**SAR workflow** — draft, review, and file Suspicious Activity Reports directly in the partner portal. Auto-generated narratives are drafted from transaction flag evidence; analysts edit and file through a supervised workflow.

**Compliance calendar** — schedule KYC refresh reminders, AML review deadlines, and rescreening events per entity.

Full API reference: [Compliance API](/en/partners/api-reference/compliance)

## AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

Protegey acts as a critical **intelligence layer** in your AML stack.

### Transaction Monitoring

Protegey provides real-time risk scores that can be fed into your Transaction Monitoring System (TMS).

- **Benefit**: Reduce false positives by excluding low-risk entities validated by the network.
- **Benefit**: Catch "cleaning" phases where money moves through mule accounts before exiting the system.

### SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) Support

Our detailed reason codes and network correlation data provide the evidence needed to file high-quality, defensible SARs.

## KYC (Know Your Customer) & CIP

Protegey supplements your Customer Identification Program (CIP).

- **Identity Verification**: We do not replace document verification (e.g., scanning a passport). Instead, we provide **Identity Reputation**.
- **The Gap**: A passport can be genuine but stolen. Protegey tells you if that genuine passport has been used to open 50 accounts in the last hour.

## Regulatory Cooperation

We actively engage with regulators to advocate for collaborative fraud defense.

### Information Sharing (USA Patriot Act 314(b))

For US partners, Protegey facilitates information sharing under Section 314(b) safe harbors.

- We provide the secure channel and structured data formats necessary to comply with FinCEN requirements.

### PSD2 / SCA (Europe)

Protegey's Transaction Risk Analysis (TRA) capabilities help partners utilize exemptions to Strong Customer Authentication (SCA).

- **Low Risk Analysis**: Real-time scoring allows you to route low-risk transactions through frictionless flows, improving user experience while remaining compliant.

## Audit Readiness

In a regulated environment, "because the algorithm said so" is not an acceptable answer. Protegey ensures you are always audit-ready.

### Explainability

We do not use "black box" deep learning for regulatory decisioning. Every risk score is accompanied by:

- **Reason Codes**: Human-readable explanations (e.g., `VELOCITY_HIGH`, `PREVIOUS_FRAUD_HISTORY`).
- **Contributing Factors**: Breakdown of what drove the score (weighted factors, confidence levels).
- **Risk Factor Details**: Granular breakdown showing specific values that triggered each factor.

### Immutability

Every decision is logged and immutable.

- **Decision Logs**: You can retrieve the exact state of the world (inputs, scores, logic) at the moment a decision was made, even years later.
- **Model Governance**: We maintain strict version control of all risk models. You will never be scored by an "unknown" model version.
- **Tamper-Proof**: Audit logs are cryptographically signed and append-only (no deletions or modifications).

**Partner Roles**:

- **Administrator**: Full access, user management, billing
- **Analyst**: Case management, investigation, decisions
- **Viewer**: Read-only access to dashboards and cases
- **API User**: Programmatic access only (no portal)

### Auto-STR Correlation

When `structuring` and `velocity_breach` AML flags co-occur within 72 hours on the same entity, Protegey automatically drafts a Suspicious Transaction Report. This eliminates the risk of a compliance team missing the co-occurrence pattern during high-volume periods. Auto-drafted SARs require explicit review and filing — they are never automatically submitted to regulators.

### Multi-Currency FATF Thresholds

The AML engine applies per-currency thresholds calibrated to FATF-equivalent local values for 20+ African and major currencies. Thresholds cover single-transaction reporting, 24-hour structuring aggregation, and 1-hour velocity breach. Currency-specific limits are available via `GET /api/v1/partner/compliance/geo-risk/{countryCode}`.

### OSINT Confidence Scoring

OSINT screening results include a 4-dimension confidence score: Authority (40%), Freshness (25%), Corroboration (20%), Specificity (15%). Low-confidence matches (< 0.3) are routed to manual review rather than contributing to auto-block decisions — reducing false positives from stale or weakly-corroborated OSINT sources.
