## Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Signal** | A raw data point sent to Protegey (e.g., Device ID, IP Address). |
| **Session** | A continuous period of user activity grouped by a session identifier. |
| **Verdict** | The final outcome of the decision engine (ALLOW, BLOCK, CHALLENGE). |
| **Partner Portal** | The dashboard where you manage keys, policies, and view analytics. |
| **Environment** | Isolated contexts for Testing (Sandbox) and Live (Production). |

## FAQs

### What happens if the API is down?
Our SDKs are designed to fail open. If the Protegey API is unreachable, your application should default to "ALLOW" (or your preferred fallback) to prevent blocking legitimate users.

### Can I test in Production?
No. Please use the Sandbox environment for all testing. Production traffic affects your billing and your risk profile.

### How often are risk scores updated?
Real-time. As soon as we receive a signal, the risk score is recalculated based on the latest global intelligence.
