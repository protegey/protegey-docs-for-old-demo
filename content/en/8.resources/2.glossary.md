Definitions of core terms used throughout the Protegey platform and documentation.

## Core Concepts

### Signal

A discrete indicator of risk submitted by a partner. Example: "User ID 123 committed payment fraud."

### Entity

The subject of a signal or query. Common entities include `User`, `Device`, `IP Address`, `Email`, `Phone Number`, and `Payment Instrument`.

### Risk Score

A normalized value from 0-100 indicating the probability that an entity is fraudulent. Higher is riskier.

### Trust Score (Partner Trust)

An internal metric representing the reliability of a partner. Partners with high accuracy and uptime have higher Trust Scores, giving their signals more weight in the network.

## Fraud Typologies

### Synthetic Identity

An identity assembled from real and fake information (e.g., real SSN + fake name) to create a "new" credit file.

### Account Takeover (ATO)

Unauthorized access to a legitimate user's account, often via credential stuffing or phishing.

### Bust-Out (First-Party Fraud)

A legitimate user who establishes credit with the specific intent of maxing out limits and defaulting.

### Velocity Abuse

High-frequency activity (signups, transactions) within a short time window, characteristic of bot attacks.

## System Terms

### Sandbox

An isolated environment for testing integration. Data in Sandbox is synthetic and does not affect Production.

### Idempotency

The property that an operation can be applied multiple times without changing the result beyond the initial application. (Note: Many Protegey write operations are _not_ idempotent by default to track velocity).

### False Positive

A legitimate user/transaction incorrectly flagged as fraud. We strive to minimize this rate (<1%).

### False Negative

A fraudulent user/transaction incorrectly accepted as legitimate.

## KYB Terms

### KYB (Know Your Business)

A verification process for onboarding business customers. Partners collect structured information, supporting documents, and run AML screening before approving an applicant to use their services.

### KYB Questionnaire

A versioned, configurable intake form composed of typed questions organised into sections. Questionnaires are **locked** automatically once the first invite is sent — after that, clone to create a new version.

### Invite Token

A 64-character cryptographically random token embedded in the applicant portal URL. Single-use, expires in 30 days. The applicant does not need a Protegey account to complete the form.

### Screening Identifier

A flag (`is_screening_identifier: true`) on exactly one question in a KYB questionnaire that designates the answer (typically the registered business name) as the entity name for AML screening on approval.

### Application Status

The lifecycle state of a KYB application:
`draft` → `submitted` → `under_review` → `approved` / `rejected` / `more_info_required`

`approved` and `rejected` are terminal. `more_info_required` allows the applicant to resubmit.

### EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence)

Elevated compliance scrutiny automatically triggered when a KYB application is approved and the entity meets one or more risk thresholds: `risk_score > 0.75`, `is_pep = true`, or a sanctions match. Creates a high-priority Case Management entry for manual review.
