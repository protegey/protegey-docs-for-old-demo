Protegey's effectiveness depends on partner signals being submitted in good faith. This page defines acceptable use, describes how we detect abuse, and outlines the consequences for misuse.

## Acceptable Use

Access to Protegey is gated to verified financial institutions, fintechs, telcos, and regulatory bodies. By accepting the Partner Agreement, partners commit to using the platform exclusively for:

- Submitting genuine fraud intelligence signals from their own customer interactions
- Querying risk scores for the purpose of protecting their customers from fraud
- Operating within the rate limits and quotas assigned to their plan

## What Constitutes Misuse

### Signal Manipulation

Submitting signals that do not reflect genuine fraud observations — including fabricated events, inflated severities, or coordinated signals designed to suppress competitor entities — constitutes manipulation of the shared intelligence network.

**Why this is serious**: Every partner's fraud signals influence risk scores for all network participants. Manipulated signals degrade the accuracy of collective intelligence and may cause legitimate customers across multiple institutions to be incorrectly flagged.

### Competitive Targeting

Using Protegey to submit signals against known customer identifiers of competitors, or to query risk scores for market intelligence rather than fraud protection, is prohibited.

### API Reconnaissance

Systematically probing the API to map detection boundaries, threshold values, or scoring behaviour — including through automated scripts, randomised payload variations, or high-frequency testing — is a breach of the Partner Agreement.

### Data Harvesting

Using risk query responses or webhook payloads to build or enrich data sets unrelated to fraud protection, or sharing API responses with third parties not covered by the Partner Agreement, is prohibited.

### Credential Sharing

Sharing API keys across unrelated organisations, or using a single partner account to submit signals on behalf of entities not covered by the onboarding agreement, is not permitted.

## How We Detect Misuse

Protegey monitors partner API activity continuously. Detection mechanisms include:

**Behavioural anomaly monitoring**
- Sudden changes in signal submission patterns (volume, event type distribution, entity type mix)
- Queries submitted for entities with no plausible relationship to the partner's business domain
- High query-to-signal ratios (querying without ever contributing)

**Statistical outlier detection**
- False positive rates significantly above the partner network average trigger review
- Entity identifiers appearing in signals without corresponding business context
- Signal timestamps inconsistent with real-time transaction processing

**Canary entity monitoring**
- Synthetic entity identifiers seeded into the network; any signal referencing them indicates enumeration or harvested data
- Canary triggers escalate to immediate manual review

**Rate limit abuse patterns**
- Repeated rate limit breaches followed by backoff and retry may indicate automated boundary-probing behaviour

## Trust Score Impact

Misuse indicators directly affect a partner's **Partner Trust Score**, which determines how much weight their signals carry in the network:

| Behaviour | Trust Score Impact |
|---|---|
| High false positive rate | Reliability factor reduction |
| Rapid velocity without business context | Shadow mode activation |
| Canary trigger | Immediate manual review + possible weight zeroing |
| Confirmed manipulation | Suspension + potential permanent ban |

Partners in **Shadow Mode** continue to submit signals, but those signals are weighted at zero — they do not influence network intelligence. This allows investigation without alerting the partner.

## Consequences

| Violation | Response |
|---|---|
| Unintentional misuse (integration error) | Notification + guidance to fix |
| Negligent misuse (poor credential hygiene) | Warning + mandatory rotation |
| Deliberate signal manipulation | Immediate suspension |
| Competitive targeting or data harvesting | Termination + potential legal action |
| Network-wide damage (confirmed) | Termination + regulatory notification |

Serious violations may be referred to relevant financial regulators and law enforcement.

## Reporting Suspected Misuse

If you observe behaviour that suggests another partner is misusing the network — such as signals that appear to target your customers — report it to [security@protegey.com](mailto:security@protegey.com).

Reports are treated confidentially and investigated by our Trust & Safety team. Confirmed misuse by any partner triggers immediate action to protect all network participants.

## Good Faith Partners

Partners operating in good faith have nothing to fear from these controls. Monitoring is designed to surface anomalies that cannot be explained by legitimate fraud detection activity. Standard integration patterns, including burst signal submission after database catchup, scheduled batch queries, and testing in Sandbox, are all recognised and excluded from misuse analysis.

If you are uncertain whether a planned integration pattern constitutes misuse, contact [support@protegey.com](mailto:support@protegey.com) before implementing it.
