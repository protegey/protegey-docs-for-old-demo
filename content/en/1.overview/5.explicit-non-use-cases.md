---
title: Explicit Non-Use Cases
description: What Protegey is NOT designed for
---

To ensure legal compliance and system integrity, we are explicit about what Protegey is **not** designed for. Attempting to use the platform for these purposes is a violation of the Partner Agreement.

## 1. Not a Background Check Service

Protegey analyzes **risk signals**, not personal histories.

- **DO NOT** use Protegey to screen potential employees.
- **DO NOT** use Protegey for tenant screening.
- **DO NOT** use Protegey to determine insurance eligibility.

These activities are governed by the FCRA (in the US) and similar laws globally, which require specific dispute resolution mechanisms that Protegey's risk model does not support.

## 2. Not a Marketing Data Source

Protegey data is strictly for **fraud prevention and security**.

- **DO NOT** use Protegey signals to target marketing campaigns.
- **DO NOT** use Protegey to build customer profiles for advertising.
- **DO NOT** use Protegey to "enrich" lead data for sales teams.

## 3. Not a Sole Determinant for Adverse Action

While Protegey provides a powerful risk score, it should be one factor in a broader decisioning matrix.

- **DO NOT** automatically deny service based solely on a Protegey score without a human review or multi-factor policy (unless the score indicates a confirmed blocklist match).
- **DO NOT** use Protegey as the exclusive reason for closing an existing account without internal investigation.

## 4. Not for Personal surveillance

- **DO NOT** use the system to look up individuals for personal reasons.
- **DO NOT** query the system for celebrities, public figures, or acquaintances unless they are engaging in a legitimate transaction with your institution. All queries are audit-logged and monitored.

## 5. Not a "Black Box" Oracle

Partners are responsible for their own risk decisions. Protegey provides intelligence, not indemnification. Partners must understand _why_ a signal was flagged and ensure their use of that signal complies with their own internal policies and local laws.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="red"}
**Enforcement**: We employ active monitoring to detect misuse. Violation of these non-use cases will result in immediate suspension of connectivity and potential legal action.
::
