# What is Protegey?

Protegey is an infrastructure-grade fraud intelligence platform that enables regulated institutions to collaboratively detect and prevent fraud. It operates as a secure signal exchange, allowing partners to share risk indicators without exposing sensitive customer data.

## The Problem: Asymmetric Warfare

Fraudsters operate in networks. They share tools, techniques, and stolen identities. They attack multiple institutions rapidly, exploiting the time lag in information sharing between defenders.

Defenders, conversely, typically operate in silos. A bank seeing a synthetic identity has no easy way to warn a fintech that is about to open an account for the same identity. This asymmetry gives fraudsters a significant advantage.

## The Solution: Collective Defense

Protegey rebalances this equation by enabling **collective defense**.

### 1. Unified Intelligence

When a partner flags an entity (e.g., a device, email, or identity) as high-risk, that intelligence is instantly normalized and incorporated into the network's risk models.

### 2. Privacy-Preserving Architecture

We use advanced cryptographic techniques and data minimisation strategies to ensure that intelligence sharing does not violate privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA) or competitive secrecy. Partners share _signals_, not customer profiles.

### 3. Real-Time Decisioning

The platform provides sub-second risk scores and signals, allowing partners to block fraudulent transactions or applications in real-time, before losses occur.

## How It Works

1. **Ingest**: Partners submit signals about confirmed or suspected fraud events via API.
2. **Fuse**: Protegey aggregates these signals with network-wide history and third-party data.
3. **Score**: Our models calculate a risk score and confidence level.
4. **Distribute**: Partners query the API for risk assessments on new interactions.

## Key Differentiators

- **Trust-Based Network**: Access is restricted to vetted, regulated institutions.
- **Reciprocity**: Participation requires contribution. Freeloading is structurally prevented.
- **Adversarial Resilience**: The system is designed to resist reverse engineering and gaming.
