To protect the performance and integrity of the network, we enforce limits on API usage.

## Rate Limits

Limits are applied per **API Key**.

| Environment    | Tier         | Requests / Minute | Burst (5sec) |
| -------------- | ------------ | ----------------- | ------------ |
| **Sandbox**    | All          | 100               | 20           |
| **Production** | Starter      | 1,000             | 100          |
|                | Professional | 5,000             | 500          |
|                | Enterprise   | Custom            | Custom       |

### Handling Rate Limits

- If you exceed the limit, you will receive `429 Too Many Requests`.
- The response headers include:
  - `X-RateLimit-Limit`: Your limit.
  - `X-RateLimit-Remaining`: Requests left in the window.
  - `X-RateLimit-Reset`: Timestamp when the window resets.

## Data Constraints

| Resource | Field       | Constraint                      |
| -------- | ----------- | ------------------------------- |
| Signal   | `metadata`  | Max 5KB (JSON)                  |
| Signal   | `entity_id` | Max 255 characters              |
| Batch    | `items`     | Max 100 items per batch request |

## Fair Usage Policy

The Protegey network relies on reciprocity.

- **Query/Signal Ratio**: We monitor the ratio of Queries (consuming) to Signals (contributing).
- **Target Ratio**: ~1:1 (ideal).
- **Acceptable**: up to 10:1 (10 queries for every 1 signal).
- **Abusive**: > 100:1 (Freeloading).

Partners who consistently consume intelligence without contributing back may have their access reviewed or throttled.
