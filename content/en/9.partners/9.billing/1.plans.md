Protegey uses a **dual-layer pricing model**: a subscription plan for platform access, and per-call API metering for usage-based billing. You pay for the platform tier you need plus the API calls you make.

---

## Core API Pricing

API calls are billed per-call, tiered by monthly volume:

| API | 1 – 100k calls/mo | 100k – 500k calls/mo | 500k+/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fraud / Account Check | $0.08 | $0.06 | Custom |
| Risk Scoring (Assess) | $0.07 | $0.05 | Custom |
| Device / Behavioral Signals | $0.06 | $0.04 | Custom |
| Identity Verification | $0.10 | $0.08 | Planned (Phase 11) |

### Risk Intelligence Bundle

Partners using all three active APIs (Account Check + Risk Scoring + Device) at consistent volume can qualify for a bundled rate. Contact your account manager or [reach out to us](#) to discuss bundle pricing.

## Billing FAQ

**When does API usage billing start?**
API calls are counted from your first production request. Sandbox calls are never billed.

**How are overages handled?**
Usage above your plan's included API quota is billed at the standard per-call rate for your tier. You will receive a usage alert at 80% and 95% of your quota.

**Can I switch plans mid-cycle?**
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated). Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

**What happens if I exceed my seat limit?**
Additional seats can be added on a per-seat basis. Contact your account manager or upgrade your plan.

**Are Identity Verification calls included in the bundle?**
Identity Verification (Phase 11) is not yet available. When launched, it will be priced separately and optionally included in bundle negotiations.
