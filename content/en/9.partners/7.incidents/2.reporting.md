# Reporting Incidents

Effective incident response relies on speed.

## How to Report

### Security Issues
Send an encrypted email to **security@protegey.com**.
- If possible, sign/encrypt messages with our [PGP Key](/compliance/pgp-key.asc).
- **Subject**: `[SECURITY] - {Short Description}`.

**Include**:
- Time of incident (UTC).
- Logs or evidence (redact PII).
- Impact assessment.

### Operational Issues
Open a ticket in the **Partner Portal** or email **support@protegey.com**.
- **Subject**: `[OPS] - {Environment} - {Short Description}`.

## What We Need from You

When reporting, provide:
1. **Request IDs**: The `req_...` ID from the API response header.
2. **Environment**: Sandbox or Production?
3. **Reproduction Steps**: CURL command (masking keys) that reproduces the error.

## Joint Response

In a SEV-1 event, we may request a "War Room" channel (Slack/Zoom) with your technical lead to coordinate remediation.
