# Escalation Matrix

If a critical incident is not being resolved within SLA, use this escalation path.

## Level 1: Support Desk

- **Contact**: support@protegey.com
- **Role**: Initial triage and routing.
- **SLA**: 1 hour response (SEV-2+).

## Level 2: Duty Manager

- **Contact**: (Available in Partner Portal)
- **Role**: Incident Commander.
- **Trigger**: SLA breach or SEV-1.

## Level 3: Executive Escalation

- **Contact**: cto@protegey.com
- **Role**: Strategic oversight.
- **Trigger**: Extended SEV-1 outage (>4 hours) or Major Security Breach.
