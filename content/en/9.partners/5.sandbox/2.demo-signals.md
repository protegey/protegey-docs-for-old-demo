# Demo Signal Generation

If you don't have real signal data yet, you can use our **Demo Signal Generator** to populate your sandbox with realistic scenarios.

## How to Generate Signals

1. Navigate to **Sandbox > Demo Signals** in the Partner Portal.
2. Select a **Signal Template** (e.g., Account Takeover).
3. Choose a **Volume Preset** (5, 20, or 100 signals).
4. Click **Generate Signals**.

## Available Templates

| Template | Scenario |
|----------|----------|
| **Account Takeover** | Simulates suspicious logins and unauthorized account activity. |
| **Stolen Financials** | Simulates transactions using compromised payment methods. |
| **Synthetic Identity** | Simulates onboarding with fake or manipulated identity data. |
| **Phone Verification Failure** | Simulates repeated failures in 2FA/KYC flows. |
| **External Fraud Report** | Simulates incoming reports from external partner networks. |

## Rate Limits

To ensure system stability, demo signal generation is limited to **3 requests per hour** per partner. For higher volume testing, use the [API Simulator](./api-simulator).

## Viewing Results

Once generated, signals will appear in your **Signal Logs** with a `Simulator` source tag. You can filter the logs by environment and source to isolate demo data.
