---
title: Go-Live Requirements
description: Mandatory steps before production access
---

To ensure the safety of the network, all partners must complete this readiness review before being granted Production access.

## 1. Technical Readiness

- [ ] **Integration Tested**: Successfully submitted signals and queries in Sandbox.
- [ ] **Error Handling**: Confirmed application fails gracefully on API errors.
- [ ] **Security**: API keys are securely managed (not hardcoded).
- [ ] **TLS**: Using TLS 1.2+ for all connections.

## 2. Operational Readiness

- [ ] **Team Trained**: Fraud analysts understand how to interpret Protegey scores.
- [ ] **Process Defined**: Clear SOP for what to do when a user is flagged "High Risk".
- [ ] **Contacts**: Emergency contact details provided in Portal.

## 3. Compliance & Legal

- [ ] **Contracts Signed**: MSA and DPA executed.
- [ ] **Privacy Update**: Your privacy policy covers fraud prevention data sharing.
- [ ] **Auth Check**: If using 2FA/MFA, integration with Protegey signals is mapped.

## Final Approval

Once these items are complete, go to **Partner Portal > Settings > Production Access** and click **Request Access**. Our team will review your account within 24 hours.
