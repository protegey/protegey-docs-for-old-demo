Signal intelligence is the art and science of extracting meaningful risk indicators from raw data points. Protegey moves beyond simple "lists" (blacklists/whitelists) to dynamic, context-aware intelligence.

## The Signal Hierarchy

We categorize intelligence into three tiers:

### Tier 1: Hard Signals (Facts)

Objective, verifiable data points.

- "This device has a rooted OS."
- "This IP is a known TOR exit node."
- "This phone number is invalid."

### Tier 2: Behavioral Signals (Patterns)

Derived from activity over time.

- "This identity is signing up for 50 accounts per hour."
- "This device is associated with 20 different emails today."
- "This user types at 1000 characters per minute (bot behavior)."

### Tier 3: Network Signals (Reputation)

Derived from the collective wisdom of the network.

- "3 different banks have flagged this entity for synthetic fraud."
- "There is a 95% correlation between this email provider and credit losses."

## Signal processing Pipeline

When an entity (User, Device, IP) is queried, Protegey processes it through a multi-stage pipeline:

1. **Extraction**: Identify all keys (email, phone, device ID).
2. **Expansion**: Find related entities (e.g., other accounts linked to this device).
3. **Retrieval**: Fetch signal history from the hot storage layer.
4. **Scoring**: Apply the Trust Engine's weighting algorithms.
5. **Decisioning**: Compare against dynamic thresholds to determine the final risk level.

## The Output: Actionable Decisions

Instead of overwhelming partners with raw data, we provide actionable outputs:

- **Risk Score**: 0-100 score indicating probability of fraud.
- **Risk Level**: `Low`, `Medium`, `High`, `Critical`.
- **Reason Codes**: Specific reasons for the score (e.g., `VELOCITY_HIGH`, `NETWORK_BLOCK`).
- **Recommendation**: `APPROVE`, `REVIEW`, `DECLINE`.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="blue"}
**Integration Note**: Partners can map these outputs directly to their own decision engines to automate accept/reject workflows.
::

## Signal Decay

Intelligence is not static. A "bad" IP address today might be reallocated to a legitimate user tomorrow.

Protegey implements **intelligent signal decay**:

- Signals lose weight over time.
- Different entity types decay at different rates (e.g., IPs decay faster than emails).
- Positive behavior (successful transactions) can rehabilitate a reputation.

This ensures the network remains accurate and fair.

## OSINT Confidence Scoring

OSINT screening results are scored across four dimensions before contributing to risk decisions:

| Dimension | Weight | What It Measures |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| Authority | 40% | Regulatory credibility of the source (UN SDN = 1.0, regional advisory = 0.55) |
| Freshness | 25% | Exponential time-decay since last ingest (half-life ~253 days) |
| Corroboration | 20% | Number of independent sources confirming the match |
| Specificity | 15% | Precision of match (name + DOB + nationality vs name only) |

Low-confidence scores (< 0.3) are surfaced for manual review rather than contributing to automated decisions.

## Weighted Fusion Output

Every signal response includes a `fusion` breakdown showing how the final score was computed:

- `behavioral_score` (35%) — NUFSS detector aggregate
- `network_score` (40%) — hot graph 2-hop propagated risk
- `contextual_score` (15%) — resolver enrichment features
- `historical_score` (10%) — 90-day rolling entity average

The `fused_score` (0.0–1.0) is multiplied by 100 to produce the final `risk_score` in the response.
