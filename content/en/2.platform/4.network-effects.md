Protegey is designed as a **network effect platform**. The value of the platform scales exponentially with the number of active partners.

## The Data Flywheel

1. **More Partners** join the network.
2. **More Signals** are ingested covering more entities.
3. **Better Intelligence** is generated from the richer dataset.
4. **Higher Catch Rates** are achieved for all partners.
5. **Reduced Fraud Losses** attract even more partners.

## Types of Network Effects

### 1. Cross-Industry Visibility

Fraudsters often hop between verticals. They might test a stolen card at a retailer, use the identity at a neo-bank, and then try to get a loan from a traditional lender.

Protegey connects these dots. A signal from a retailer can protect a bank, and vice versa.

### 2. Velocity Blind Spot Elimination

A fraudster opening 1 account at 10 different banks looks like a normal customer to each individual bank.
To Protegey, they look like an identity opening 10 accounts in 1 hour.

The network effect eliminates the "velocity blind spot" that exists between institutions.

### 3. Adversarial Cost Increase

As the network grows, the cost of attacking it rises. A fraudster cannot simply "burn" an identity at one bank and move to the next. Once an identity is burned anywhere in the network, it is burned everywhere.

This forces fraudsters to burn through resources (stolen identities, clean IPs, devices) much faster, destroying their ROI.

## Participation Incentives

To drive these network effects, Protegey's model incentivizes sharing:

- **Reciprocity**: Partners must contribute to consume.
- **Trust Weighting**: Partners who provide high-quality signals gain higher trust scores, giving them more influence in the network.
- **Feedback Loops**: Partners who confirm fraud (feedback loop) help train the models for everyone, and receive better tailored scores in return.

## Graph Intelligence

Network effects in Protegey extend beyond aggregated signal sharing to real-time graph traversal. The hot graph engine:

1. Maps entity relationships through shared attributes (device fingerprints, IP addresses, phone numbers, co-occurring transactions)
2. Propagates risk scores from confirmed fraud actors outward through their network — 1-hop neighbors receive 70% of the propagated risk, 2-hop neighbors receive 49%
3. Amplifies the network score by up to +15 points when a direct neighbor has `risk_score > 80`

This means a previously low-scored entity that shares a device fingerprint with a confirmed fraud actor will immediately receive an elevated network risk score — even before any suspicious transaction from that entity is observed.

**Fraud ring detection**: A cluster of entities sharing multiple edge types (device + IP + co-occurrence) is a strong fraud ring signature. The graph visualization endpoint (`GET /entities/{uuid}/graph-visualization`) renders these clusters as force-graphs in the partner portal.
