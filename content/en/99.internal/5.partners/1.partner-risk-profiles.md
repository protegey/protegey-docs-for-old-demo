# Partner Risk Profiles

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lock" color="red"}
**RESTRICTED**: Internal Protegey team only.
::

We categorize partners into risk profiles to manage network hygiene.

## Risk Categories

### Low Risk (Green)

- **Trust Score**: > 0.8
- **Characteristics**: Large established banks, verified fintech unicorns.
- **Privileges**: Instant signal propagation, higher rate limits.

### Medium Risk (Yellow)

- **Trust Score**: 0.5 - 0.8
- **Characteristics**: New partners, smaller lenders.
- **Privileges**: Standard limits. Signals may require 1 confirmation before impacting network scores.

### High Risk (Red)

- **Trust Score**: < 0.5
- **Characteristics**: Partners with high false positive rates or erratic API usage.
- **Privileges**: "Shadow Mode" (signals ingested but zero-weighted). Manual review required for upgrade.

## Manual Overrides

T&S Ops can apply overrides in the Admin Dashboard:

- `TRUST_BOOST`: Temporarily increase trust (e.g., after successful audit).
- `TRUST_PENALTY`: Temporarily decrease trust (e.g., during investigation).
- `KILL_SWITCH`: Immediately revoke all keys and reject signals.
