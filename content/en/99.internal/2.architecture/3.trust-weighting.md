# Trust Weighting

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lock" color="red"}
**RESTRICTED**: Internal Protegey team only.
::

The **Partner Trust Score** (0.0 - 1.0) determines how much influence a partner's signal has on the network.

## The Formula

```python
trust_score = (base_trust * reliability_factor * volume_bonus) - penalties
```

### Components

1.  **Base Trust**: Defaults to `0.5` for new partners.
2.  **Reliability Factor**:
    - Calculated as `1 - (false_positive_rate * 2)`.
    - Capped at `1.5` max multiplier.
3.  **Volume Bonus**:
    - `log10(monthly_signals) / 10`.
    - Incentivizes high contributors.
4.  **Penalties**:
    - Manual overrides applied by T&S team for compliance violations.

## Updating Weights

Trust scores are recalculated **nightly at 02:00 UTC** via the batch job `CalculateTrustScores`.
