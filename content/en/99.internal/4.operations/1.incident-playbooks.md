# Incident Playbooks

## SEV-1: System Outage

1.  **Ack**: Acknowledge PagerDuty alert within 5 mins.
2.  **Status Page**: Update `status.protegey.com` to "Investigating".
3.  **Rollback**: If caused by recent deploy, rollback immediately. Do not debug forward.
4.  **Comms**: Notify CSMs for Enterprise partners.

## SEV-1: Data Leak

1.  **Isolate**: Revoke keys of compromised partner or shut down affected localized ingress.
2.  **Assess**: Determine scope (what data, how many records).
3.  **Legal**: Contact Legal Counsel immediately.
4.  **Evidence**: Preserve logs. Do not restart servers if memory dump is needed.

## Partner Trust Decay

If a partner's False Positive rate spikes > 10%:

1.  **Suppress**: Enable "Shadow Mode" for that partner (signals stored but weight set to 0).
2.  **Contact**: Reach out to partner technical contact.
3.  **Review**: Analyze last 100 signals.
