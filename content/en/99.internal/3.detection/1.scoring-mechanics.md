# Scoring Mechanics

::callout{icon="i-lucide-lock" color="red"}
**RESTRICTED**: Internal Protegey team only.

## NUFSS Pipeline (as of v1.3.0)

The **Network-Unified Fraud Severity Score** is computed in `FraudScoringService::calculateScoringBreakdown()` via these components:

```
severity_score = base_risk × source_confidence × anomaly_factor
```

| Component | Source | Range |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| `base_risk` | Taxonomy Registry lookup by `event_type` (+ detector boost) | 0.3 – 1.0 |
| `source_confidence` | Signal `source_confidence` field | 0.0 – 1.0 |
| `anomaly_factor` | `AnomalyDetectionService` multiplier | 1.0 – 1.5 |

The full breakdown is stored in `signals.metadata['scoring_breakdown']` and surfaced to partners via the alert lineage endpoint.

### Detector Severity Boost

When fired detectors overlap with a taxonomy entry's `detectors_required`, the `base_risk` receives a proportional boost (up to +0.10):

```php
$boost = min(0.10, $matchRatio * 0.10);
$baseSeverity += $boost;
```

Where `$matchRatio = count(intersection) / count(detectors_required)`.

## Legacy Base Score (entity risk, pre-NUFSS)

The entity risk score ($S$) uses a normalized sigmoid of the weighted signal sum ($W$):

$$
S = \frac{100}{1 + e^{-(W - T)}}
$$

Where:
- $W$ = Sum of (Signal Severity × Partner Trust × Time Decay)
- $T$ = Threshold constant (currently `5.0`)

## Time Decay

Signals decay exponentially over time:

```python
decay = e ^ (-0.1 * days_since_event)
```

- **Half-Life**: ~7 days.
- **Max Age**: 90 days (effectively 0 weight).

## Tuning Policy

Changes to $T$ (Threshold), Decay Constant, or taxonomy base severities require **CTO approval** and a full backtest against the `golden_dataset`.
