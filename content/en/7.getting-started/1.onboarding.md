# Partner Onboarding

Joining the Protegey network is a curated process. Because our effectiveness relies on the quality of intelligence shared, we rigorously vet every partner.

## The Journey

### Stage 1: Discovery & Evaluation
_Current State_

You are exploring this public documentation to determine if Protegey fits your needs.
- **Action**: Review [Overview](/overview), [Platform Architecture](/platform), and [Compliance](/compliance).
- **Goal**: Confirm strategic fit.

### Stage 2: Application
_Duration: 1-3 Days_

Submit your application to join the network.
- **Requirement**: You must be a regulated entity or verified business.
- **Check**: We verify your corporate identity and regulatory status.
- **Outcome**: Invitation to the Sandbox Environment.

### Stage 3: Sandbox Integration
_Duration: 1-2 Weeks_

You receive access to the **Partner Portal** and **Sandbox API**.
- **Access**: Sandbox API keys issued.
- **Docs**: Full technical documentation unlocked (Sandbox scope).
- **Task**: Integrate the API into your staging environment.
- **Validation**: Pass a series of "Integration Health Checks" (e.g., successfully submitting signals, handling error responses).

### Stage 4: Production Approval
_Duration: 2-5 Days_

Once technical integration is verified, we finalize the partnership.
- **Legal**: Sign the Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Data Processing Agreement (DPA).
- **Security**: Complete a lightweight security questionnaire.
- **Outcome**: Production API keys issued.

### Stage 5: Live & Monitoring
_Status: Live Partner_

You are live in production.
- **Phase-In**: We recommend starting in "Shadow Mode" (logging scores without blocking) for 1 week.
- **Tuning**: We work with you to calibrate risk thresholds.
- **Graduation**: Full enforcement mode.

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
1. **Technical Team**: Developers to handle API integration.
2. **Operational Team**: Fraud analysts or Trust & Safety managers to consume intelligence.
3. **Legal Authority**: Ability to sign data sharing agreements.
