# Data Lifecycle

Centry manages data with a strict lifecycle policy to ensure security, privacy, compliance and relevance.

## 1. Ingestion & Minimization

Data enters the system via the Signals API.
- **Ingress Validation**: Strictly typed schemas reject malformed data.
- **PII Hashing**: Sensitive identifiers (like account numbers or government IDs) are hashed immediately upon entry if not already hashed by the partner.
- **Transient Processing**: Raw paylods are processed in memory and discarded; only the normalized signals are persisted.

## 2. Active Utility (Hot Storage)

For the period where data is most predictive (typically 30-90 days), it resides in high-performance hot storage.
- **Purpose**: Real-time scoring and velocity checks.
- **Access**: Sub-millisecond retrieval by the decision engine.
- **Encryption**: Encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

## 3. Aging & Decay

As signals age, their impact on risk scores decays mathematically.
- **Decay Function**: Signals lose influence according to type-specific decay curves.
- **Rehabilitation**: New positive signals can accelerate the decay of old negative signals.

## 4. Archival (Cold Storage)

After the active utility period, data moves to cold storage.
- **Purpose**: Model training, trend analysis, and compliance auditing.
- **Access**: Batch processing only; not accessible for real-time queries.
- **Anonymization**: Aggregated and stripped of granular identifiers.

## 5. Deletion (Purge)

Data reaches its end of life based on retention policies.
- **Standard Retention**: 7 years (aligned with banking audit requirements) for aggregated risk data.
- **PII Retention**: Strictly limited (often purged much sooner, e.g., 1-2 years depending on type/jurisdiction).
- **Right to Erasure**: We support GDPR/CCPA "Right to be Forgotten" requests, propagating deletions across our systems.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-shield-check" color="green"}
**Compliance**: Our data lifecycle is automated and audited to ensure compliance with global data protection regulations.
::
